# Sample Storyline: Digital Transformation Strategy for RetailCo

## Context
RetailCo is a mid-sized retail chain with 150 stores across the Northeast United States.
After two years of declining foot traffic and flat online sales, the CEO has commissioned
a strategic review to define a digital transformation roadmap.

## Key Messages

1. **The retail landscape has fundamentally shifted**
   - E-commerce penetration has accelerated 5 years ahead of projections post-pandemic
   - Customer expectations for omnichannel experiences are now table stakes
   - Competitors who invested early in digital are capturing market share

2. **RetailCo is falling behind on critical digital capabilities**
   - Legacy POS systems limit inventory visibility and customer insights
   - E-commerce platform is outdated with 35% cart abandonment rate (vs. 20% benchmark)
   - No unified customer data platform - CRM fragmented across 4 systems
   - Store associates lack tools for clienteling and endless aisle

3. **We recommend a phased transformation over 3 years**

   Phase 1 (Year 1): Foundation
   - Implement unified commerce platform
   - Deploy modern POS with clienteling capabilities
   - Establish customer data platform

   Phase 2 (Year 2): Acceleration
   - Launch mobile app with loyalty integration
   - Enable BOPIS/BORIS across all stores
   - Implement AI-powered personalization

   Phase 3 (Year 3): Innovation
   - Deploy computer vision for inventory
   - Launch AR/virtual try-on
   - Establish marketplace model

4. **Investment of $45M required with 3.2x ROI over 5 years**
   - Technology: $28M
   - Change management: $8M
   - Contingency: $9M
   - Expected benefits: $144M NPV from revenue growth and cost savings

5. **Critical success factors**
   - Executive sponsorship and cross-functional governance
   - Phased approach to manage risk and build capabilities
   - Strong change management and associate training
   - Partner with proven implementation partner

## Ask
Approve the recommended roadmap and $45M investment, with quarterly progress reviews
and stage-gate checkpoints between phases.

---
Target Audience: Board of Directors, CEO, CFO
Duration: 30 minutes
Tone: Executive, data-driven, action-oriented
